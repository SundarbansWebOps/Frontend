# **PART I — OUR STORY AND VISION**

# **1\. Our Vision**

**Build a Sundarbans that solves the problems students face—the problems that were never addressed before.**

We wanted to build a Sundarbans that was more than just another house in the IITM BS ecosystem. We wanted to create a student-driven ecosystem that identified the real challenges students faced and developed practical solutions to address them.

We believed that a student body should not limit itself to organising events. It should understand the problems students face, identify gaps in the existing ecosystem, and build solutions that make their lives better.

# **2\. Everything Starts With Why**

When students enter the **IITM BS degree programme**, they come with **expectations, ambitions, and goals**.  
They want to **make new friends**, build meaningful relationships, **achieve good academic scores, explore events, participate in activities, and enjoy their student life.**  
Some want to prepare for placements and secure good job opportunities. Others want to prepare for GATE or UPSC alongside their undergraduate degree. Many simply want a reliable place where they can ask questions, find resources, seek guidance, and get answers to their queries.

These expectations are natural. Every student wants to make the most of their degree.  
However, the online nature of the IITM BS programme created several challenges. Students were part of a large academic community, but they did not always have access to the social connections, structured guidance, resources, and support systems they needed.

We identified these gaps and decided to work on them.  
Our major objectives were:

1. **Connect students:** Help students make friends and connect with other IITM BS students living in their cities.
2. **Improve academic support:** Provide subject-wise communities, notes, previous-term papers, and opportunities for collaborative learning.
3. **Improve placement preparation:** Build a structured, end-to-end placement preparation ecosystem.
4. **Support GATE aspirants:** Provide structured guidance and a dedicated community for students preparing for GATE.
5. **Promote Generative AI:** Build a dedicated GenAI club and community to provide students with better exposure to this emerging field.
6. **Make the community safe and secure:** Introduce a WhatsApp verification system to improve accountability and member safety.
7. **Make information accessible:** Build an AI assistant that could answer students' common queries.
8. **Build a central digital platform:** Bring the services, resources, communities, and initiatives of Sundarbans together through a full-stack website.
9. **Keep students informed:** Introduce a newsletter to communicate updates and opportunities.
10. **Encourage participation:** Give members opportunities to propose and organise meaningful initiatives.

It all started with the problems students were facing and our commitment to solving them.  
We had a clear vision, a structured roadmap, and a determination to build solutions rather than simply discuss problems.

# **PART II — OUR INITIATIVES**

# **3\. StudyBuddy — The First Social Media Platform of IITM BS**

## **The problem**

One of the first challenges students faced in an online degree was making new friends and building connections outside the virtual classroom.  
At that time, the IITM BS ecosystem had a student community of more than 30,000 students across the pan-BS programme, but there was no dedicated platform to help students discover and connect with other students living in the same city.

For example, a student living in Prayagraj might want to meet other IITM BS students in the city, make friends, study together, or build a local student community. However, there was no dedicated platform that made this process easy.  
Paradox was one of the existing opportunities to meet other students, but regular offline meetups had not yet been introduced to the degree community. Meetups were introduced in March 2024\.  
Students could be living in the same city, pursuing the same degree, and working towards similar goals without knowing one another.  
We saw this as a genuine problem that needed a solution.

## **Our vision**

To connect students living near one another, help them build friendships, and make the IITM BS student community stronger.

## **Our solution — StudyBuddy**

We built StudyBuddy, the first social media platform of the IITM BS degree, to help students discover and connect with fellow IITM BS students living in the same city or nearby areas.  
Through StudyBuddy, students could:

- Discover other students living nearby.
- See the approximate distance between themselves and other students.
- Find fellow students in their city.
- Connect with other students through LinkedIn, Instagram, Snapchat, and other social platforms.
- Build friendships and local student networks.
- Find peers with whom they could study, collaborate, and share experiences.

The purpose was simple: help students discover other IITM BS students living around them and make it easier to connect with those students.  
StudyBuddy was not just another technical project. It was a solution to a problem created by the online nature of the degree.  
We wanted students to have the opportunity to build meaningful friendships and local communities despite studying online.  
We identified the gap, built the platform, and gave students a way to connect with peers who were already around them.

# **4\. Academic Support — One Place for Academic Resources and Communities**

## **The problem**

One of the most challenging aspects of the IITM BS degree was studying alone.  
Students often struggled to find the right people, resources, and communities to help them understand difficult concepts and prepare for examinations.  
Although the Discourse forum was available, many students did not prefer it as their primary platform for academic discussions. One of the reasons was limited familiarity with its user interface.  
Other third-party platforms and communities also existed, but there was no unified, student-body-led ecosystem bringing together subject-wise communities, academic resources, and peer support.  
Students needed a place where they could ask questions, solve doubts, discuss concepts, prepare for examinations together, and access useful learning resources.  
We wanted to solve this problem comprehensively.

## **Our vision**

To make Sundarbans a single destination where students could find the academic resources and peer support they needed.

## **Our solution**

We established multiple subject-wise student communities where students could:

- Ask questions and clarify academic doubts.
- Help other students understand difficult concepts.
- Discuss subject-specific topics.
- Prepare for examinations together.
- Share knowledge and learning resources.
- Collaborate with peers pursuing the same subjects.

Alongside these communities, Sundarbans collaborated with TheOpenNotes to provide subject-wise notes and previous-term examination papers.  
This brought together three important elements of academic support: learning resources, examination preparation material, and student communities.  
If students needed notes, Sundarbans was there.  
If students needed previous-term papers, Sundarbans was there.  
If students needed subject-wise student communities, Sundarbans was there.  
If students wanted to ask questions, solve doubts, or prepare for examinations with peers, Sundarbans was there.  
Sundarbans was the only house offering this combination of academic resources and subject-wise student communities as a unified support ecosystem.  
We wanted to reduce the effort students spent searching across disconnected platforms and make academic support easier to access.

If students needed notes, past exam papers, or subject-wise study groups, Sundarbans provided a unified support ecosystem.

# **9\. WhatsApp Verification System — Making Sundarbans Safe and Secure**

## **The problem**

At that time, Sundarbans had a large WhatsApp student community divided primarily into three major groups.  
However, the identities of the people joining through particular phone numbers were not systematically verified.  
Anyone could potentially join using a random number without providing reliable identifying details.  
If someone behaved inappropriately in a group or contacted another member through direct messages, administrators could struggle to identify the person responsible. Without information such as a verified name, email address, or roll number, reporting the incident to the IITM BS team could become difficult.  
The same problem existed across other houses.  
We believed that a large student community was not enough. It also needed a reliable process for verification and accountability.

## **Our vision**

To make the Sundarbans WhatsApp community safe and secure for everyone.

## **Our solution — A semi-automated WhatsApp verification system**

After understanding the problem, we decided to build a robust, semi-automated WhatsApp verification system.  
We developed the first and most advanced WhatsApp verification system in our degree ecosystem, designed to improve the safety and accountability of the student community.  
Implementing the system required a difficult decision.  
We closed all three major Sundarbans WhatsApp groups because their members had not been systematically verified. We then created a fresh Sundarbans WhatsApp community with 100% verified members.  
Although rebuilding the community was difficult, we believed that safety and accountability were more important than maintaining large groups without reliable identity verification.  
With verified membership information, including names and email addresses, we could identify members and escalate serious incidents to the IITM BS team through the appropriate channels.  
This gave us a stronger basis for reporting misconduct and addressing issues involving members of the community.

## **Why this mattered**

A few months later, we observed an inappropriate message posted from an unknown number in another house's WhatsApp group.  
Unfortunately, the administrator's immediate options were limited to deleting the message and removing the number from the group because there was no verification system available to identify the person behind it.  
This reinforced our belief that community safety required preparation and accountability, not merely reacting after something went wrong.  
Sundarbans was the first house to build a 100% verified WhatsApp community.  
We believed that preventing problems was better than waiting for them to occur.  
Our belief: Early preparation is the best protection against challenges.

# **11\. Sundarbans AI Assistant — The First AI Assistant of IITM BS**

## **The problem**

When freshers join the IITM BS degree, they have numerous questions about the degree, courses, fees, Paradox, houses, clubs, societies, groups, and other aspects of student life.

To make answers to common questions about the IITM BS degree and its student ecosystem easier and faster to access.

## **Our solution — The Sundarbans AI Assistant**

We built the first AI assistant of IITM BS to help students resolve their common queries.  
The assistant was integrated into the Sundarbans website so students could visit the platform, ask questions, and receive answers about the degree and student community.  
Instead of writing an email to the support team for every basic question, searching across multiple platforms, or asking a senior and hoping for a response, students could approach the assistant directly.  
Our objective was to make information more accessible and reduce the effort required to resolve routine queries.  
We did not build the assistant merely because AI was becoming popular. We built it because students had questions that needed answers, and we saw an opportunity to make that process faster and easier.  
Sundarbans was the first and only house to develop its own AI assistant to resolve students' queries.

# **PART IV — OUR FUTURE ROADMAP**

# **16\. The Sundarbans Startup Series — From Idea to Scaling**

While we were already working on the Placement Series and GATE Series, our next major plan was to launch a comprehensive Startup Series.  
We wanted to extend our structured-support approach to students interested in entrepreneurship.  
Many students have ideas and ambitions, but building a startup requires much more than an idea. Students need to understand how to identify a problem, validate a business opportunity, conduct market research, build an MVP, acquire customers, generate revenue, raise funds, register a company, access incubation, and scale a business.  
We wanted to create an entire series covering the journey from idea to scaling.

## **Our vision**

To build an environment where aspiring student entrepreneurs could learn the fundamentals of building a startup through structured sessions delivered by experienced entrepreneurs and industry leaders.

## **The proposed roadmap**

1. **Idea Generation and Validation:** Identifying meaningful problems, developing ideas, validating assumptions, and determining whether a proposed solution addresses a real need.
2. **Market Research:** Understanding the target market, researching customer needs, analysing competitors, and identifying business opportunities.
3. **Building an MVP:** Converting an idea into a minimum viable product, testing it with real users, gathering feedback, and validating demand.
4. **Product Development:** Understanding product design, development, iteration, user feedback, and the process of building something customers genuinely want.
5. **Marketing and Growth:** Learning positioning, branding, customer acquisition, marketing strategies, and business growth.
6. **Sales:** Understanding sales processes, customer conversations, lead generation, closing customers, and revenue generation.
7. **Fundraising:** Learning about pitch decks, investor outreach, funding options, fundraising strategies, and the fundraising process.
8. **Company Registration and Legal Foundations:** Understanding company incorporation, registration requirements, compliance basics, and the legal foundations of running a startup.
9. **Incubation:** Learning how to identify suitable incubators, apply for incubation programmes, and access startup resources and support.
10. **Scaling:** Understanding how to expand a customer base, build teams, develop operations, grow revenue, and scale a business sustainably.

## **Learning from experienced founders**

The Startup Series was inspired by the approach of Y Combinator and other startup-learning ecosystems that help founders understand the process of building a company.  
Our plan was to have sessions delivered by CXOs of Indian unicorn startups, bringing practical insights from people with experience building and scaling businesses.  
We wanted students to learn from experienced leaders rather than rely only on theoretical explanations.  
Our ambition was to build a complete startup-learning ecosystem:  
From idea to MVP. From MVP to customers. From customers to revenue. From early traction to scaling.  
We wanted Sundarbans to become a place where aspiring entrepreneurs could find the knowledge, guidance, and environment needed to take the first steps towards building their own startups.
